📦 StockPile Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

Short version: StockPile keeps everything you enter on your own phone. We do not collect, receive, sell or share any of your data. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

What StockPile stores, and where

  • Your books (money in and out, stock, debts, customers, budgets, settings) are saved in your phone's browser storage, inside the StockPile app.
  • Receipt photos you take are saved on your phone, the same way.
  • If you set a PIN, your books, backups and receipt photos are encrypted on your phone with keys only your PIN, recovery key or security answers can open.
  • Nothing you enter is sent to us or to anyone else. We have no server that receives your data.

Permissions

  • Camera: used only when you choose to scan a barcode or photograph a receipt. Photos stay on your phone. Receipt text is read on your phone, not online.
  • Files and sharing: used only when you choose to save a backup, open a backup, bring in a bank statement, or share a receipt, report or backup through your phone's share menu. You choose where these go.

Sharing between your own phones

You can share books with another phone by sending a locked file, or by linking two phones on the same Wi-Fi. The link connects the two phones directly. Updates are encrypted with a sharing password you choose and do not pass through any StockPile server.

Messages to customers

Reminder and receipt messages are opened in your own texting or email app for you to send. StockPile does not send them itself.

Data collection

StockPile does not collect personal information, device identifiers, location, contacts or usage statistics. The app's files are downloaded from GitHub Pages, which may keep standard web server logs (such as IP addresses) as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can erase everything from inside the app, or by uninstalling StockPile or clearing its storage in your phone's settings. Because we never receive your data, we cannot recover it for you. Keep backups.

Children

StockPile is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. A future optional paid cloud sync feature would come with an updated policy before it is turned on.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/Stockpile-apk/StockPile-/issues.
